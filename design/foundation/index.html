Body text stays fixed — no fluid type in an app. The Arabic build pairs Literata with Noto Naskh Arabic and Plex Sans with IBM Plex Sans Arabic; nothing else changes. Numbers in columns are tabular; numbers in a sentence are not.

The spread. A margin of 7.5–12rem where the product speaks, a gap of 2rem, and a reading column of 38rem — about 61 characters of the serif. Page gutters are 1–2.5rem, fluid. Below 45rem the margin folds above its column.

Nothing in the system says left or right. Inline-start and inline-end only, so the Arabic build mirrors without a redraw.

  1. If it is in the serif, it came from the learner or their material. The product never speaks in the serif.
  2. The learner’s words are the largest text on any page they appear on. The margin is never louder than the column.
  3. The second ink marks what can be pressed and what proves an answer. Nowhere else.
  4. No red, no amber, no green-as-correct. A gap is unfilled; an error is a sentence in ink.
  5. A citation is never smaller than the sentence it supports, and is one tap from the source.
  6. “Your material doesn’t cover this” is an answer, in the product’s voice, with what appears to be missing.
  7. Before the learner has written, nothing of theirs is shown — and the emptiness is explained.
  8. No scores, streaks, badges, predictions or celebrations. The evidence strip has three stages and no number.
  9. Rules of 1px, never shadows. Radii of 3, 6 and 10. Not everything is in a box; almost nothing is.
  10. Logical properties only. 44px controls, 52px on touch. Every control has a visible focus ring.
